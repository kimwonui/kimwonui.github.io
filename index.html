About

01 / Profile

I am an economics student at Penn State and an independent builder. I use AI throughout my work: exploring ideas, prototyping products, implementing changes, and connecting the tools I use every day.

My projects include GilLog, a route-based diary that brings walks, photos, and shared memories onto a map, and KeyGlo, a Korean–English translation keyboard. I also lead creative projects through IC and research fitness markets for SpoAny.

I want to join a team where AI is part of how everyone works. I bring curiosity, ownership, and a habit of turning ideas into something people can try. My 18 months in the ROK Marine Corps shaped how I work with a team and follow through.
